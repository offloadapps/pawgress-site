Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026. Applies to the Pawgress app for Android and the Pawgress web version.

The short version: Pawgress does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your tasks and pals are saved only on your own device.

What Pawgress stores, and where

Everything you add to Pawgress is saved on your device and nowhere else. That includes your tasks, reminders, settings, pals and progress. We (the developer) never receive a copy and cannot see it.

  • Android app: saved in the app's private storage on your phone. The app does not have permission to use the internet.
  • Web version: saved in your browser's local storage on that device.
  • Backup codes: if you make a backup code in Settings, it stays on your device unless you choose to copy or send it somewhere yourself.

Deleting the app, clearing its data, or using Settings > Start over removes everything.

Android backup

If you have turned on Google backup on your phone, Android may include Pawgress's save in your phone's backup, so your progress comes back on a new phone. That backup is made and stored by Google under your Google account and Google's privacy policy. We cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Permissions the Android app uses

  • Notifications: to show the reminders and pal updates you choose in Settings. You can turn them off at any time.
  • Run at startup: to set your reminders again after your phone restarts.

Speak (voice input)

When you tap Speak, Pawgress asks your phone's own speech recognition service to turn what you say into text. That service is provided by your phone's maker or Google, not by Pawgress, and works under their privacy terms. Pawgress only receives the finished text, which is saved on your device like any other task. Pawgress never records or keeps audio.

Sharing into Pawgress

If you share text to Pawgress from another app, it opens in the Offload box on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The website

This website and the web version are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic technical information, such as IP addresses, when pages are loaded. See GitHub's privacy statement. The website itself uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Children

Pawgress is made for teens and adults. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If Pawgress ever starts collecting any data, this policy will say so clearly before that happens.
