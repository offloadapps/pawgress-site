How it works

Three steps. No setup, no account.

1

Offload it all

Type it how you think: "bins every friday 6pm, remind me 30 mins before". Dates, repeats, reminders and sizes are worked out for you. You can even say it out loud.

2

Your day, sorted

Today first, then what's coming up, with gentle reminders. Every finished task earns hearts for your pal and energy for errands.

3

Grow a pal, stick on stickers

Hearts help your pal grow up. Errands bring back coins for stickers, cosy rooms and holidays.

Gentle by design

Made to help on hard days, not nag on good ones.

No streaks

Nothing expires and nothing is ever late. Your pal is always happy to see you.

Small steps count

Tiny tasks pay off, and "Just start" sits with you for five minutes.

Private and free

No account and no ads. Your list stays on your phone.
