How it works

Three steps, no setup and no account.

Offload it all

Type it how you think: "bins every friday 6pm, remind me 30 mins before". Dates, repeats, reminders and sizes are worked out for you.

Your day, sorted

Today first, then what's coming up, with gentle reminders. Every finished task earns hearts for your pal and energy for errands.

Grow a pal, stick on stickers

Ten pals to raise, drawn by the developer's kids. Coins from errands buy stickers, cosy rooms and holidays.

Gentle by design

Made to help on hard days, not to nag on good ones.

No streaks

Nothing expires and nothing is ever late. Your pal is always happy to see you.

Small steps count

Tiny tasks pay off, and "Just start" sits with you for five minutes.

Private and free

No account and no ads. Your list stays on your phone.

On an iPhone?

Open the web version in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It works offline and saves on your phone. Reminders need the Android app for now.
