Offload it all

Type it how you think: "bins every friday 6pm, remind me 30 mins before". Dates, repeats, reminders and sizes are worked out for you.

Today first, then what's coming up, with gentle reminders. Finished tasks earn hearts for your pal and energy for errands that earn coins.

Gentle by design

No streaks. Nothing expires and nothing is ever late. No account, no ads, and everything stays on your phone.

On an iPhone?

Open the web version in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It works offline and saves on your phone. Reminders need the Android app for now.
